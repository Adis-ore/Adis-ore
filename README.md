## ⚛️ About Me

I'm **Oreoluwa (Adigun Oreoluwa Akintomiwa)**, a **final year Physics and Engineering Physics student** at **Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU), Ile-Ife, Nigeria**. I build **mobile apps and web apps**, and I'm deeply interested in **energy** and **aviation (aircraft and drones)**.

- 🎓 Final year, Physics and Engineering Physics @ OAU
- 📣 PRO of **PEPSA** (Physics and Engineering Physics Students Association)
- 🛢️ Involved with **SPE OAU** (Society of Petroleum Engineers, OAU chapter)
- ⚡ Interested in **energy**: oil and gas, power systems, and the future of clean energy
- ✈️ Interested in **aviation and aerodynamics**: how planes fly, drones, and the physics behind them
- 🧠 Long-term focus: **Quantum Machine Learning for energy**
- 💻 I build web and mobile apps for real clients and real problems
- 🌍 Lagos, Nigeria
- 💼 Open to collaborations, freelance projects and opportunities
- 🏷️ Building a public brand as **ADISDTECHI**

Energy is where my physics meets real-world impact. I'm interested in petroleum and power systems, energy efficiency, and how emerging tech like quantum computing and machine learning can make energy smarter and safer, especially for Nigeria and Africa.

## ✈️ Aviation & Aerodynamics

Aviation is where physics takes flight. I'm interested in **aerodynamics**, the study of how air moves and how it acts on objects moving through it, and in the four forces of flight: **lift, weight, thrust and drag**. I'm exploring how aircraft and drones work, and how code, sensors and physics come together in flight systems.

`Aviation` · `Aerodynamics` · `Drones / UAVs` · `Flight Physics` · `Fluid Dynamics`
